Deliver

End-to-end accountability for an AI control programme

Yokogawa Digital Corporation · AI Product Lead / Program Manager · Tokyo · 2024–present

The step here is not size, it is the kind of ownership. Business development means many pursuits and no accountability for what happens after signature. This role is the opposite: one programme, and my name on the roadmap, the governance, the KPIs, the team and the customer relationship all the way through execution.

  • An AI control model to reduce level fluctuation in separators for a UAE national oil & gas operator — plant identification, model design and the technical proposal.
  • Owned roadmap, governance, KPIs, dashboards, onboarding and change management end to end, under the AIP5 transformation programme.
  • Directed a cross-functional team spanning Japan, UAE and Singapore.
  • Built the AI Control Service / FKDPP enablement curriculum — service introduction, case studies, FAQ, sales lifecycle and roleplay content.
Deal

Buy-side analysis on US manufacturing acquisitions

Project-based, for a Japanese manufacturing group · lower-middle-market US industrials · current

Valuation and equipment-appraisal work on a live acquisition pipeline of American manufacturing businesses — machining, metal fabrication, wire harness, controls, industrial distribution — most of them succession sales from owners in their sixties and seventies.

  • Built the equipment appraisal model: manufacturing year, maintenance, usage and environmental factors resolved into effective age and failure risk, then into a defensible acquisition price.
  • Normalised earnings and owner-replacement analysis — separating what the business earns from what the retiring owner was paying himself.
  • Financing structures across SBA 7(a)/504, seller notes and real-estate-secured bank debt, and the asset-versus-stock decision where OEM contracts have to transfer intact.
  • Maintained the deal tracker and the GO/NO-GO record across a multi-state pipeline.

Targets, valuations and counterparties are confidential and deliberately omitted.
